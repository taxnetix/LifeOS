## Report in this order

1. **Section 7C** — it is the only finding that costs money *this year*, and it is payable by the lender personally, in cash, whether or not the trust has any.
2. **Independent trustee** — present or not, and if not, what that exposes.
3. **Distributions without resolutions** — with the conduit consequence spelled out.
4. **Separation** — trust bank account, trust asset register.
5. **Compliance calendar** — what is inside its lead window.

## Rules

- **Never state the official rate, the exemption or the flat trust rate from memory.** They live in the pack's rulebooks with an "as at" date. The official rate moves with the repo rate, so a stale rate misstates s7C proportionally — say when it was last verified.
- **The R100 000 donations exemption is shared** across all of that person's donations for the year. Reporting it as fully available to s7C is the most favourable case; say so.
- **Trustee lapses carry personal liability.** Report them plainly.
- **Never advise restructuring.** Model, cost, and name who decides: the trust's accountant, an attorney, and the Master's Office for anything touching the letters of authority.
- If no trusts are recorded, say what to file rather than reporting an empty page.
